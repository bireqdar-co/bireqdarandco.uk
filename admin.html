ACCESS & ROLES

Team

The owner sees everything. Catalogue team accounts can only add, edit and remove products, upload images and change stock — they cannot see orders, customers, enquiries, analytics or settings. Every account signs in with a password and an authenticator code.

Recovery codes

Recovery codes let you sign in if your authenticator app is unavailable. Creating new codes replaces any you saved before.

Move to a new phone

Removes your current authenticator and signs you out. You will scan a new setup key at your next sign-in.
