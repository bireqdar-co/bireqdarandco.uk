CLIENT CARE · YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy notice.

Last updated 16 November 2026

BIREQDAR & CO. respects your privacy. This notice explains how we use personal information when you browse our website, create an account, place an order, or contact us. We use information only for the purposes described here and take reasonable steps to protect it.

Who is responsible

Data controller: BIREQDAR & CO. (trading name). For privacy questions or requests, please use our contact form and mention “Privacy request”.

Information we collect

  • Accounts: name, email address, password hash, email verification status, age confirmation timestamp, saved delivery details, saved wishlist pieces, account activity and session information.
  • Google sign-in (when enabled): Google shares your verified email address, name and account identifier so we can create or access your client account. We do not request or store your Google password or profile picture.
  • Orders: name, email, telephone number, delivery address, items ordered, order value, delivery and payment status.
  • Enquiries and bespoke requests: contact details and the message or design details you provide, which may include style, metal, carat, size, weight and reference images.
  • Website operation and security: technical request information such as IP address and browser details may appear in hosting and security logs.

Payment card details are entered directly with Stripe during hosted checkout. We do not store full card numbers or security codes on our servers.

How and why we use it

  • To create and manage client accounts, save delivery details at your request, keep a wishlist and protect account access (contract and our legitimate interests).
  • To respond to enquiries and prepare requested bespoke designs (steps requested by you before a contract, and our legitimate interests in providing client service).
  • To process, deliver and support orders, including payment, tax and accounting records (contract and legal obligations).
  • To send transactional messages about order receipt, payment confirmation and dispatch (contract and service communications).
  • To protect the website, prevent abuse and troubleshoot service issues (legitimate interests and legal obligations where applicable).
  • To send a password reset message when you request one (account service and security).

We do not currently use this website to send marketing emails or run advertising analytics.

Service providers and transfers

We use service providers to host the application and database, and to provide payment and transactional email services when those features are enabled. The application is hosted in Oregon, United States, and the database is hosted in Frankfurt, Germany. Stripe processes payments through its hosted checkout; Resend may deliver password-reset, account-verification and order-update emails if configured. If you choose Google sign-in, Google Identity Services verifies your sign-in and shares the account details described above. These providers process information under their service terms and security measures. Contact us if you would like more information about applicable international transfer safeguards.

Cookies and local storage

We use a strictly necessary session cookie to keep you signed in and local storage in your browser to remember your shopping bag. These are used to provide services you request. The site does not currently set advertising or analytics cookies.

How long we keep information

We keep account information while your account is active and for a reasonable period after closure to handle requests and protect against disputes. Order and transaction records are kept for the periods required by tax, accounting and other applicable laws. Enquiry details are retained while we respond and for a reasonable period afterwards, unless a longer period is needed for a commission, legal claim or legal obligation. Security logs are retained according to the hosting provider’s operational retention settings.

Your choices and rights

Depending on the circumstances, UK data protection law may give you the right to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or transfer of your information, to object to certain processing, or to withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. To make a request, use our contact form. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

Changes and contact

We may update this notice as our services change. The latest version will be published on this page. For questions, please contact us through the BIREQDAR & CO. contact form.
